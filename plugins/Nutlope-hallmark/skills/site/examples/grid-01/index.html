three weeks, forty rooms, one grid

basel · 5 to 26 june 2027

exhibitions

forty rooms across five buildings hold the printed work: two hundred posters from thirty countries, hung on a single modular system that repeats in every room.

the hanging grid is drawn on the walls in pencil and left visible. what you see is the system and the work at once, which is the point of the festival.

tickets

one pass opens every venue. evenings cover the screenings only. students and anyone under 26 enter free with id, all three weeks.
