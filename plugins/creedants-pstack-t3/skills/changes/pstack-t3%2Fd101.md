- `brigade.py pass record` puts every review round on record with `--report`, `--member`, and `--late`, and `close` warns about a review report with no row. A dish in progress, in review, sent back, or blocked owes a round decision at three send-backs written after its last passing verdict and its last answered round decision. While it owes one, `dish`, `brief`, and `fire` refuse another fix round on it. `86 add --dish <id> --round-budget` writes the decision, and an answer of known limits, redesign, or drop restarts the count.
